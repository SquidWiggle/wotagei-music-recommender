Glossary:
- wotagei (ヲタ芸): fan light-stick dance performed to idol songs
- 打ち曲 (uchi-kyoku): songs suited to wotagei
- takamari (高まり): the rise in intensity/tension into the chorus
- AB melo -> sabi: verse (A melody), pre-chorus (B melody), chorus (サビ)
- MIR: music information retrieval

   Investigate how "takamari" (高まり, the rise in intensity into the chorus in
   songs like Mizuki Nana's) can be measured. Check MIR literature on chorus
   detection and build-up/tension curves, plus wotagei community discussions of
   how 打ち曲 are chosen (AB melo -> サビ structure, BPM range, energy curve).

   pop/EDM/rock tracks too.
